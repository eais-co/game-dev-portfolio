ABOUT // 02
02

I'm a game developer and UI/UX designer who likes working at the point where systems, interfaces, and stories meet. In Unity and C#, that means building the mechanics and gameplay logic that make a world feel alive; in Figma, it means shaping the screens and flows that make it feel usable.

I care about the same thing on both sides of that line: does this feel intentional? A menu should read as clearly as a level should play. That's the standard I hold my own work to, whether I'm scripting a game system or laying out an interface.
